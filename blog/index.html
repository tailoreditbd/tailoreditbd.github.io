SEO

Build a stronger foundation for search visibility

Write titles and headings that describe each page accurately. Answer the questions your customers ask in clear language, and avoid repeating the same content across several pages.

Check that search engines can access important content and that internal links lead to relevant destinations. Review search performance over time to identify content and technical improvements.

Web development

What to plan before building a business website

List the pages and information customers need before contacting you. Gather your logo, service descriptions, contact details, and work samples. Give each page a clear primary action.

Plan for mobile navigation, content updates, and ongoing maintenance. Before launch, check your links, contact options, and layouts across different screen sizes.

UI/UX design

Make your website easier for customers to use

Group related information and use headings that help people scan the page. Give buttons clear labels, keep navigation consistent, and make contact details easy to reach.

Check text contrast, keyboard navigation, and touch target sizes. Ask someone unfamiliar with your site to find a service, then use their feedback to remove confusing steps.
